It has been a little while. Over the past few months I have been mulling over the concept of **focus** and how it was framed by Cal Newport in his decade old book called **Deep Work**. In truth, I was not planning to write a reflection on this book, but I found my brain reaching for the concepts Newport laid out in his book when studying for the CISSP. If you're in the security industry, you know how dreadful the CISSP exam can be. Thankfully, I passed.

You will likely find hundreds of articles already on how to pass this difficult exam. This article is not about that. This is more for the busy industry professional that is looking to take the exam, already has the raw materials, but needs to effectively use them.

The concepts laid out in Cal Newport's book are agnostic to the goal(s) you are trying to achieve. I'm not crazy on productivity books on the main fact that they emphasize on **optimizing** which I frankly think that hurts more than it helps. But Newport has an approachable way to relay these concepts beyond the typical advice.
