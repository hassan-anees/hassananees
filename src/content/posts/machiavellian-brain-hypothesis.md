I first came across the Machiavellian brain hypothesis when reading the psychology section within Security Engineering by Ross Anderson. The hypothesis states that we got smarter for the sake of deception (I am oversimplifying here). This idea is not widely accepted but its a very interesting way of looking at things. 

Typically, I have learned that our brains evolved to become more cognitively efficient (smarter) for the purpose of socialization. That empathy allows understanding which leads to collaboration and aids in our ability to survive. You may have already heard this line of thought before that empathy was a necessary tool to create positive relationships and those are the people who survived to pass on their genes.

The Machiavellian brain hypothesis flips this idea on its head. I found this fascinating, not because I believe this to be the truth, but rather it gave a completely new perspective on the motivation behind our actions.
